# Learn two conversational skills
No software, automation, subscription, or health advice is involved.
R1: Practice a Spanish self-introduction for 20 minutes, record it, and have the
tutor confirm all five supplied facts are understandable. This is the first session.
R2: Practice ordering a meal for 20 minutes, record the role-play, and have the
tutor confirm the order includes an item, quantity, and price question.
Use practice and feedback as the work layers. Each session stands alone. The two
different checklists are local to their skills, not a reusable software engine.
